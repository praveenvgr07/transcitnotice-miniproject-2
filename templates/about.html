About TransitNotice

A smart public transport information platform designed for Tamil Nadu, India.

Our Mission

TransitNotice centralises verified schedule information from Tamil Nadu's major public transport providers — MTC, TNSTC, SETC and CMRL — into a single, user-friendly platform that automatically detects and notifies passengers about changes that may affect their journeys.

We believe every traveller deserves timely, accurate information. Whether it's a bus rescheduling, a metro cancellation, or a new holiday service, TransitNotice monitors official data feeds around the clock so you don't have to.
